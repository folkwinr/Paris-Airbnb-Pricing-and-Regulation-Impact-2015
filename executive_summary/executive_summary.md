# Executive Summary  
## Paris Airbnb Supply & Pricing Shift After 2015

## Overview

Paris Airbnb market shows a clear shift around the 2015 regulation period.

The strongest signal is not only that the market changed, but **how** it changed:

> New host/listing growth reached its highest point in 2015, then dropped sharply. At the same time, average prices started rising again from 2015 to 2020.

This suggests a tighter market after 2015, with fewer new entries and stronger price pressure.

---

## Key Evidence

### 1. Supply growth peaked in 2015

The highest number of new hosts/listings was recorded in 2015:

| Year | New hosts/listings | Change vs 2015 peak |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2015 | **12,147** | Peak |
| 2016 | **8,867** | **-27.0%** |
| 2017 | **4,585** | **-62.3%** |
| 2018 | **4,294** | **-64.6%** |
| 2019 | **5,685** | **-53.2%** |

### Interpretation

Airbnb did not disappear after 2015, but the speed of new supply growth slowed strongly after the peak year.

This is an important market signal because short-term rental platforms depend heavily on new supply growth.

---

## 2. Prices increased after supply growth slowed

Before 2015, average prices were falling:

| Period | Average price |
|---|---:|
| 2009 | **€159.64** |
| 2014 | **€100.25** |
| Change | **-37.2%** |

After 2015, average prices increased:

| Period | Average price |
|---|---:|
| 2015 | **€103.65** |
| 2020 | **€143.52** |
| Change | **+38.5%** |

### Interpretation

The price trend changed direction after 2015.

Before 2015, Airbnb growth was expanding and average prices were falling.  
After 2015, new supply growth slowed and average prices increased again.

This pattern is consistent with a tighter market: fewer new listings entering the market and higher average prices.

---

## 3. Location remains a major pricing factor

Airbnb prices are highly different across Paris neighbourhoods.

| Neighbourhood | Average price |
|---|---:|
| Elysee | **€211.37** |
| Menilmontant | **€74.96** |

Elysee is almost **2.8x more expensive** than Menilmontant.

### Interpretation

Paris-wide average price alone is not enough for decision-making.

Neighbourhood-level analysis is important because pricing pressure is not equal across the city.

---

## Main Business Insight

The Paris Airbnb market appears to have moved through three phases:

1. **Growth phase before 2015**  
   New host/listing growth increased quickly and average prices declined.

2. **Peak year in 2015**  
   New hosts/listings reached the highest level: **12,147**.

3. **Tighter market after 2015**  
   New supply growth slowed sharply, while average prices increased from **€103.65 to €143.52** between 2015 and 2020.

---

## Stakeholder Recommendation

For city policy teams, platform analysts, or pricing teams, the key recommendation is:

> Monitor supply growth and price movement together.

Looking only at total listings or only at prices can miss the real market signal.

A stronger monitoring framework should track:

- New host/listing growth
- Average and median price
- Neighbourhood-level price differences
- Capacity-based pricing
- COVID or other external shocks

---

## Important Limitations

This analysis shows strong market patterns, but it does not prove that regulation caused the changes.

Other factors may also affect the market:

- Tourism demand
- Seasonality
- COVID-19
- Economic conditions
- Market saturation
- Neighbourhood popularity

Also, yearly trends are based on `host_since`, not historical booking prices.

---

## Final Takeaway

Paris Airbnb supply growth peaked in 2015 and slowed sharply afterward.

At the same time, average prices increased from 2015 to 2020.

This suggests that after 2015, the market became tighter: fewer new listings entered the market, and average prices moved upward.

For decision-makers, the key lesson is simple:

> Regulation impact should be evaluated through both supply and price signals, not one metric alone.
